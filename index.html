The maker · 011

Hi, I'm Jeff.

I've been drawing portraits of strangers at Singapore markets for eight years under the name Playing With Pencil. This booth is what happened when I asked: what if nostalgia and AI could share the same strip?

Every sketch I do is two things at once: a likeness, and a memory. I wanted a photobooth that did both — the ritual of film your parents remember, with AI that can grade it in Kodak gold or paint you into a masterpiece. Hardwood tripod, brass fittings, a model trained on forty years of photographs. Six months of tinkering later, Playing With Photo was born.

I still show up to every event myself. If you book the booth, you're booking me too — complete with terrible jokes and pencil smudges on my fingers.

Questions · 12

Before you book.

How much does a photo booth cost in Singapore?

Packages start at S$750 for 2 hours, S$880 for 3 hours (our most booked option), and S$1,380 for 6 hours. Every package includes setup, an attendant, unlimited prints, digital files, GIFs, our full AI style library and a sticker pack — no hidden extras. Custom activations get a separate quote.

What AI styles can guests choose from?

Every package includes our full AI style library — think Renaissance, Studio Ghibli, Monet and more — layered on top of a classic vintage film filter. Guests pick their style, we handle the rest. Want something beyond the library — a fully custom direction for your event? Tell us your theme and we'll make it happen, free.

How much space do you need for the setup?

About 2.5m × 2.5m, plus a power outlet nearby. That's usually all it takes — let us know your venue layout when you enquire and we'll confirm it fits.

Do you handle setup and teardown?

Yes — that's included in every package. Our attendant arrives ahead of your event start time, has everything ready to go, and breaks it all down after, so it's zero extra work for you or your planner.

Can you do a custom backdrop for our event?

Yes — custom backdrops are available on request, designed to match your event's theme and colours. Additional pricing applies depending on size and design; tell us your vision and we'll send samples and a quote.

Do you travel outside Singapore, like to Malaysia?

Yes, we take bookings across Singapore and Malaysia. Reach out with your event location and date and we'll confirm availability and any travel details.

Can we combine the photo booth with a live sketch artist?

Yes — Playing With Pencil, our live wedding sketch artist service, can be bundled with the photo booth at a 10–15% combo discount when you book both together.

How do guests get their photos?

Everyone gets unlimited prints on the spot, plus a digital copy and GIF delivered by QR code scan — no app, no waiting.

How do we book, and what's the deposit?

A 50% deposit via PayNow secures your date. We'll go through package details, AI styles and any custom add-ons before your event, and the balance is due after.
