- No local Lighthouse install; PageSpeed Insights public API returned 429 (daily
  quota exceeded for the shared/unauthenticated endpoint) — could not get an
  official Lighthouse score this run.
- Manual checks instead (curl, 3 repeated runs for consistency):
  - Page weight: index.html = 210,489 bytes (~206 KB) on the wire.
  - Load timing: TTFB ~1.4–1.5s, total ~2.4–2.5s, consistent across 3 attempts.
    Flagging this as an OBSERVATION, not a confirmed root cause — measured from this
    machine's network path to Vercel, which may not represent a real visitor's path
    (e.g. Singapore user). Needs Jeff to spot-check via pagespeed.web.dev (real

  - Render-blocking resources before first paint: 3 stylesheets loaded synchronously
    (creation.css 21KB + hero-scrub.css 12.7KB + vision-scrub.css 9KB = ~43KB) plus
    an external Google Fonts stylesheet (also blocking). This is a known LCP-delay
    pattern and matches the backlog's existing suspicion about hero-scrub JS/CSS.
  - JS payload: app.js 27KB, bg.js 22.7KB, demo-gif.js 21.8KB, hero-scrub.js 15.9KB,
    creation.js 15.9KB, others smaller. ~150KB of JS total referenced from index.html.
- NOT changed this run: restructuring critical CSS/deferring non-essential JS. The
  site's scroll-scrub hero animation is tightly coupled to this CSS/JS, and
  reordering load behavior risks visibly breaking that animation without a human
  checking it in a real browser. This needs a careful pass with visual verification,
  not a blind autonomous edit — logged as a backlog item, not executed.

## 2. Image SEO — executed

- Content image filenames audited: `photos/occasions/wedding.webp`,
  `photos/testimonials/aesop.webp`, `photos/about/jeff.webp`, etc. — these are
  already descriptive (folder + filename convey subject). No renaming needed;
  backlog item was more pessimistic than reality. `frames/f_001.webp` ... `f_123`
  are sequential hero-scrub animation frames, not content images — renaming these
  has no SEO value (they're not independently indexable/meaningful) and risks
  breaking the JS that references them by numeric sequence, so left alone.
- `og-share.jpg` was 434,059 bytes (901×1200) — heavy for a social-preview image.
  Recompressed with `sips` at JPEG quality 50. Visually compared original vs
  quality-50 and quality-60 output directly (viewed both images) — no visible
  difference; the painterly/textured AI-portrait content hides compression
  artifacts well. New size: 259,231 bytes (−40%, still 901×1200, so `og:image:width`
  /`og:image:height` meta tags remain accurate, no meta changes needed).

### 3b. FAQ section — full draft copy + schema, ready to paste if approved

Proposed placement: new section before the footer, after the packages section.
Tone matches brand-voice.md (casual, direct, warm, no jargon, mobile-short answers).

---

**How much does a photo booth cost in Singapore?**
Packages start at S$750 for 2 hours, S$880 for 3 hours (our most booked option),
and S$1,380 for 6 hours. Every package includes setup, an attendant, unlimited
prints, digital files, GIFs, our full AI style library and a sticker pack — no
hidden extras. Custom activations get a separate quote.

**What AI styles can guests choose from?**
Every package includes our full AI style library — think Renaissance, Studio
Ghibli, Monet and more, layered on top of a classic vintage film filter. Guests
pick their style, we handle the rest. Want something beyond the library — a fully
custom direction for your event? Tell us your theme and we'll make it happen, free.

**How much space do you need for the setup?**
About 2.5m × 2.5m, plus a power outlet nearby. That's usually all it takes — let us
know your venue layout when you enquire and we'll confirm it fits.

**Do you handle setup and teardown?**
Yes — that's included in every package. Our attendant arrives ahead of your event
start time, has everything ready to go, and breaks it all down after, so it's zero
extra work for you or your planner.

**Do you travel outside Singapore, like to Malaysia?**
Yes, we take bookings across Singapore and Malaysia. Reach out with your event
location and date and we'll confirm availability and any travel details.

**Can we combine the photo booth with a live sketch artist?**
Yes — Playing With Pencil, our live wedding sketch artist service, can be bundled
with the photo booth at a 10–15% combo discount when you book both together.

**How do guests get their photos?**
Everyone gets unlimited prints on the spot, plus a digital copy and GIF delivered
by QR code scan — no app, no waiting.

**How do we book, and what's the deposit?**
A 50% deposit via PayNow secures your date. We'll go through package details,
AI styles and any custom add-ons before your event, and the balance is due after.
