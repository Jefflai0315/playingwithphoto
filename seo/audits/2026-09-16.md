**Two structural gaps, both concrete and both already partly tracked:**

1. **Review count.** Every organically-ranking competitor has 17+ reviews
   (except AI Booth SG at 2, which appears to be riding a name+category
   exact-match rather than review strength). PWP's 3 reviews is the
   clearest, most directly observable reason it isn't surfacing — this is
   no longer just the Sterling Sky "10 reviews" study from
   `external-seo-benchmarks-2026.md`, it's the literal floor this specific
   competitive set has cleared. Sharpens the existing backlog item: for
   *this* query specifically, the realistic bar looks closer to
   high-teens/20s, not just 10.
2. **"AI photobooth"/"AI photo booth" appears in most competitors' business
   name, page title, or branding** — several literally named the business
   or the page after the category term (AI Photobooth Studio, "AI
   Photobooth Singapore", "AI Face Swap Photo Booth Singapore", "AI Style
   Transformation Photo Booth"). Playing With Photo's brand name contains
   neither word. Google appears to be doing straightforward name/title
   matching for this non-branded, feature-seeking query pattern in a way
   that structurally favors these competitors regardless of content
   quality.

### What this changes about prioritization

- Confirms `photo booth singapore` (PWP position 9, per 2026-09-15 GSC
  pull) is the more winnable near-term target than `ai photobooth

## Falsifiability

If PWP's review count reaches the high teens (matching the weakest
organically-ranking competitor, Makors Events at 17) and it *still* doesn't
appear anywhere on this SERP after a few more months, reviews alone aren't
the binding constraint for this specific query and the naming/branding gap
is doing more work than estimated here — revisit before assuming more
reviews alone will fix it.
