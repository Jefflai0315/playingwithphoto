## 2. Change: sitemap `lastmod` brought in line with real content dates

Found: the homepage's `<lastmod>` was still `2026-07-20`, even though
the homepage changed substantively on 2026-09-15 (wedding/corporate copy,
2 new FAQs, WebSite schema) and 2026-09-17 (vintage copy + FAQ #12).
`catalogue/videos.html` said `2026-09-09` but gained VideoObject schema on
2026-09-15.

Google only trusts `lastmod` if it's "consistently and verifiably
accurate"; a value that's two months stale on the page with the most
changes teaches it to ignore the field entirely.

| URL | Old | New | Basis (git) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | 2026-07-20 | **2026-09-17** | `78e4bcc` vintage copy + FAQ |
| `/catalogue/videos.html` | 2026-09-09 | **2026-09-15** | `5d0f53a` VideoObject schema |
| `/software/` | 2026-07-28 | unchanged | later commits were favicon + form-field tweaks, not main content |
| `/catalogue/ai-styles.html` | 2026-09-09 | unchanged | accurate |

Validated: `sitemap.xml` parses with `xml.dom.minidom`.

Falsifiability: after merge, GSC → Sitemaps should show a "Last read"
date after the merge, and URL Inspection on `/` should show a "Last
crawl" date after 2026-09-17. If the homepage's last crawl still predates
2026-09-17 two weeks after merge, `lastmod` isn't what's limiting
recrawl — use URL Inspection → Request Indexing on `/` instead.

Process note added to the PLAYBOOK: bump `lastmod` in the same commit
as any substantive page change.
