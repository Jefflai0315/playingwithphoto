  1. Video 1 of 5 · 1:41

    Install & activate

    Get the app onto your booth PC and link it to your account.

    • A Windows 10 or 11 PC, a Canon camera with digiCamControl, a DNP printer with its driver
    • Download from playingwithphoto.com/download and install it
    • If Windows warns you: Keep, then More info and Run anyway
    • Sign in to activate, check the code matches, pick your booth
    • The booth says it's activated, with your plan and AI credits

  2. Video 2 of 5 · 2:00

    Camera & printer

    Check the camera and printer in the setup wizard before your first event.

    • Battery charged, photo mode, auto power-off off
    • Camera on USB and digiCamControl running
    • Live view looks good and a test photo is sharp
    • Your DNP printer is picked and a test sheet printed
    • Right size, nothing cut off, strips cut in two (2-inch cut on)

  3. Video 3 of 5 · 1:37

    Your event

    Name your event, add your logo, and choose prints and AI looks.

    • Event name, logo and look are set
    • Print sizes and copies per guest chosen on Home
    • AI looks on or off, and the looks you want picked
    • Extras left off unless you want them (quicker queue)
    • On Events: Create event, Make active, Download all photos

  4. Video 4 of 5 · 2:01

    Event day

    Run the booth on the day and handle the common problems.

    • Setup's last step shows everything green
    • Start booth from Home, then do one test session yourself
    • Dashboard: Print to reprint, Generate to retry an AI photo
    • Show photos on a TV if you have a screen
    • No internet? Photos and prints still work; AI needs internet

  5. Video 5 of 5 · 1:52

    Guests, gallery & credits

    What guests get, guest languages, AI credits, and where to get help.

    • The QR opens each guest's online gallery (7 days on Basic and Starter)
    • Guest language set, and guests can switch if you like
    • Credits on Home; Buy credits tops up on the website
    • Advanced settings on Home shows every option
    • Help: WhatsApp +65 8989 6901 or hello@playingwithphoto.com
