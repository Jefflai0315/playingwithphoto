      FAQ

      Questions at the gate.

      Do I need internet at the event?

      Only for AI styles, AI video and QR downloads. Capture, frames, filters and printing run on the booth machine with no connection.

      What hardware do I need?

      A Windows PC or Mac, a Canon DSLR or webcam, a DNP dye-sub printer and a touchscreen. See supported hardware; if your kit differs, mention it in the demo form.

      Can I use my own branding?

      Yes. Frames, templates, colours and guest copy are set per event on every plan. Pro and Agency also remove the Playing With Photo name from guest screens.

      How much does it cost?

      Pricing depends on the plan and how many booths you run. Book a demo and we'll send a quote; billing is arranged directly with us.

      What happens if we go over our AI allowance?

      Each plan has a monthly AI allowance, and the booth shows your usage. Classic photos and printing are never metered. Talk to us about moving up a plan if you regularly run out.

      Is this the same as hiring Playing With Photo for my event?

      No. That's our rental service in Singapore, at playingwithphoto.com. This page is for operators who want to run the software on their own booths.
